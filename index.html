Questions

Things people ask first.

What file types can I upload?

The estimate tool reads STL files, up to 50 MB. Most CAD and 3D apps can export one. If your file is in another format, email it to us and we'll take a look.

What if I only have a sketch or an idea?

Email a sketch, photo, measurements or a short description of what the part needs to do. We'll review it and tell you what's needed to get it printed.

What might change the final price?

The estimate is worked out from your file's size and the options you pick. The final quote can differ if the part needs supports, extra finishing, a different material, or changes to print well. You always see the final price before anything is printed.

Is delivery included?

The estimate covers printing. Pickup is near ASU's Tempe campus, by appointment. Local delivery around Tempe can be arranged, and we confirm the details with your final quote.

When do I approve the quote, and when do I pay?

We email the final price and ready date. Printing starts only after you approve it. You pay on pickup or by invoice.

How do I tell you where each color goes?

Pick up to four colors in the estimate tool, then use the Notes box to say which part gets which color. If anything is unclear we ask before printing. The multicolor upgrade is +$5 in the US or +₹150 in India.

What does "Strength" mean in the estimate tool?

It sets how solid the inside of your part is. Printed parts are usually not solid all the way through, which saves material and time. "Standard" suits most parts. Choose a stronger setting if the part will carry weight.

How quickly can I get my part?

Most small parts are ready in 2–4 days. Larger or multi-part orders take longer, and your quote includes a ready date. Need it within 24 hours? Rush costs 25% more, and we confirm before printing that we can make it.

Do you ship orders?

Orders are currently fulfilled locally in Tempe, by pickup or local delivery. If you need shipping, ask in your request and we'll let you know what's possible.
