Pricing and payout

Price = max(minimum, grams × quantity × rate) + multicolor fee, then + rush %. Printer payout = flat + % of the low price. The estimator and the database both use these values.

Materials

Density sets the weight estimate. Price factor multiplies the per-gram rate (1 = PLA price). Untick Offered to hide a material from new orders.
