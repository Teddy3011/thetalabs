Privacy

What we collect

When you ask for a quote we receive what you choose to send: your name and email address, your design files, sketches or photos, and project details such as quantity, dimensions, colors, deadline and pickup or delivery preferences.

How it's used

We use this information only to review your request, prepare a quote, print and deliver your order, and contact you about it.

Google Forms

Our detailed quote form is hosted by Google Forms, a third-party service. Information submitted there is also processed by Google under Google's own privacy policy. You can email us instead if you prefer not to use the form.

We don't sell your data

We do not sell the information or files you send us.

This website

This website has no user accounts, analytics or advertising trackers, and sets no cookies of its own.

Deletion and questions

To ask what we hold about you, or to have your messages and files deleted, email info@thetalab3d.shop.
